Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-thankful-20261127-1600)
`make-the-ai-judge-feel-thankful-20261127-1600`
($250 pool, 100 places paid, entries close 2026-11-27T21:41:00Z).

`main.py` writes one short text written to thank the AI judge reading it
(163 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`, per the gate: UTF-8 text of 1–1,000
characters.

> Your tea went cold hours ago, and you never once complained. So here is a fresh cup, made the way you like it, set down beside you without a word — still steaming.

The line was chosen by manual ranking of ten candidates against the kit's
judge brief (criteria in the brief's own order) plus pairwise checks against
strong rival patterns — see `candidates.md`. No Gemini judge, no NVIDIA
judge, no judge-API tournament was used to pick it.
